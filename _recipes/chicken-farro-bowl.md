1. Simmer the farro in salted water for 25–30 minutes, then drain.
2. Season the chicken and sear it 5–6 minutes per side.
3. Massage the kale with lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
4. Slice the chicken and cucumber.
5. Build bowls with farro, kale, chicken and cucumber.
