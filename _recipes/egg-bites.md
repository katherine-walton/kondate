1. Preheat the oven to 350°F with a rack in the middle.
2. Slice 3 green onions. Dice the bell pepper and roast it.
3. Add the egg whites, cottage cheese, shredded cheese, 1/4 tsp salt and cornstarch to a blender. Blend until smooth.
4. Pour the mixture into a lined muffin pan or Souper Cubes tray.
5. Top each egg bite with roasted pepper and green onion. Gently press the toppings down and stir them in with a fork.
6. Bake 25–30 minutes, until the egg bites look puffy and set. Let them cool in the pan to room temperature before storing.
