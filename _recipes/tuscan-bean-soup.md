1. If using dried beans, soak them overnight, or for 1 hour in warm water. Drain.
2. Chop the onion, carrot, celery and potato. Mince the garlic. Cut the chorizo into 1/2-inch chunks.
3. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the carrot, onion, celery, garlic and herbs and cook until fragrant, 2–3 minutes. Add the chorizo, champagne vinegar and tomato paste and cook about 2 minutes, until the tomato paste thickens.
4. Add 10 cups hot water, the potato and the beans. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat until the beans are tender and broken down and the soup has thickened, about 40 minutes (10–15 minutes with canned beans).
5. Add the greens (about 2 packed cups, chopped). Simmer, covered, until tender, about 10 minutes. Season with salt.
