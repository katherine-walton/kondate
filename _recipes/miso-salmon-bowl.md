1. Rinse the rice and start it cooking.
2. Stir the miso with a splash of water and brush it over the salmon.
3. Roast the salmon at 425°F for 10–12 minutes.
4. Thinly slice the cucumber and scallions.
5. Spoon rice into bowls and top with salmon, cucumber and scallions.
